# Credits and licences

Write the credits from the asset record kept while making the film (see
[music-sources.md](../../sound-and-loudness/references/music-sources.md)). Every asset in the film
has a source and a licence before the film is posted.

## What to use

- **CC0 or public domain** for art, textures, 3D models and sound effects wherever possible.
- **Fonts under the SIL Open Font License** (or similar): ship the licence file beside the font.
- **Public-domain art** (old paintings, maps, photographs): free to use, and still credited by
  title, artist and the collection that holds it.
- **Music:** see the sources table; prefer tracks with no Content ID registration.
- Never an asset whose licence you can't show, and never another company's product, logo or UI.

## Where credits go

- The video description on YouTube and the post body where there is room.
- A `CREDITS` section in the README or the film folder: every asset, its source URL, licence and
  required attribution.
- Attribution the licence requires goes wherever the film is published, not only in the repo.

## Licence of the toolkit's output

hivemotion is MIT and links no GPL code; H.264 comes from the system ffmpeg as a separate
program. Your film's licence is yours; the assets in it keep their own.
