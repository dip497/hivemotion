# What the evidence says about launch films

Two published studies of launch videos on X, read in October 2026: infinite.fast (194
hand-checked launches) and Flowjam (1,173 launches). Numbers are medians as reported by them;
they move over time and are confounded with account size. Treat them as direction, not law.

| Finding | Number | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Hook strength was the biggest lever | strong hook ≈ 90× the median views of the rest | Plan the first 2 s before anything else |
| A face beats a title card | face 976, product demo 332, text card 164 median views | Open on a person or the product moving; never a logo |
| Founder narration | ≈ 80% of the top decile vs 48% of all launches | A real voice helps; captions are the fallback |
| Being clearly different predicted reach | 9,385 vs 146 median views; nearly 9 in 10 videos were already clear | Lead with the one thing only this product does. "Another AI tool for X" is the costliest framing |
| Length | median 52 s; top quartile 56 s, bottom 37 s; under 15 s did worst | Master of about 52–90 s; short clips are cutdowns, not the film |
| Aspect | 16:9 ≈ 11× the median views of 9:16 in one study; the other study's winners also posted vertical | 16:9 is the master; make a vertical cut for vertical feeds only |
| Pacing | dynamic pacing ≈ 20× flat pacing; polish did not separate winners | Cut on motion; keep shots short; don't over-polish |
| Distribution | "nobody hit 1M views from a single post" | Post the film and its cutdowns on several channels the same day |

## Attacks a launch film draws in developer forums

- "What's the use case?" for any 3D or flashy view: the shot must carry information.
- "Movie parody of programming": show real work, never fake hacker motion.
- A wall of agents, windows or numbers: show one person's real day, never a count.
- Mouse-only gimmicks: show keyboard use where the audience is keyboard people.
- Machine-written copy: write posts by hand.

## Post copy that worked

- One line saying what it is, then the category, then one ask.
- Three to five short lines and one concrete technical stance.
- Show HN title: `Show HN: Name – <plain noun phrase of what it is>`.
- Product Hunt tagline: a short promise, not a feature list.
